Instructions

Welcome to The Simpsons Ultimate Quiz! Here are the instructions to get you started:

  1. Click the "Start Quiz" button on the home page to begin.
  2. You will be presented with a series of multiple-choice questions about The Simpsons.
  3. Select the answer you believe is correct by clicking on one of the options.
  4. After selecting an answer, you will receive immediate feedback on whether your choice was correct or incorrect.
  5. Your score will be updated based on your answers, and you can track your progress throughout the quiz.
  6. Once you have answered all the questions, your final score will be displayed.
  7. You can choose to retake the quiz by clicking the "Take Quiz Again" button.
  8. Don't forget to provide feedback on your experience by visiting the Feedback page!
